\fmhead[7]{PREFACE}{Preface}{preface}{PREFACE}

This book reproduces, with a few complements, a set of lectures given at
McGill University, Montreal, from Sept.~5 to Sept.~18, 1967. It has been
written in collaboration with John LABUTE (Chap.~I, IV) and Willem KUYK
(Chap.~II, III). To both of them, I want to express my heartiest thanks.

Thanks are also due to the secretarial staff of the Institute for Advanced
Study for its careful typing of the manuscript.

\medskip
\hfill \textsc{Jean-Pierre Serre}

\medskip
\hfill \textit{Princeton, Fall 1967}
